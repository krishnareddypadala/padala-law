# padala.law

LLB Exam Study Portal — Complete model answers, case laws, and study materials for 5 law papers.

**Live:** [https://padala.law](https://padala.law)

## Papers Covered

| # | Paper | Topics |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Property Law | Transfer of Property Act & Easements Act |
| 2 | Labour & Industrial Law | Trade Union Act, ID Act, Standing Orders Act |
| 3 | Company Law | Companies Act, 2013 |
| 4 | Administrative Law | Delegated legislation, natural justice, writs |
| 5 | Interpretation of Statutes | Rules of interpretation, presumptions |

## Adding Audio / Video

Place files using this naming convention — the site auto-detects them:

```
audio/{paper_id}/topic_{index}.mp3
video/{paper_id}/topic_{index}.mp4
```

Paper IDs: `property`, `labour`, `company`, `admin`, `interpretation`

See the [topic index mapping](#topic-index) below for which index maps to which topic.
